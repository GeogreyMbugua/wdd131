Parrot Media Site Plan Document

1. Site Purpose and Identity

The Objective is to provide a centralized digital platform for Parrot Media Showcasing their rich portfolio across Television, Podcasting and live media and events hosting. The site serves as both a fan base platform and a professional B2B portal for corporate brand partnerships and media bookings

2. Target Audience

The Digital Platform serves 2 distinct demographics Within the Kenyan landscape:

Primary Segment / The Listener and the Fans

Young Kenyan youths (Gen Z) who consume digital audio-first media. They engage heavily with weekly podcast content, television streams, and live social media commentary. They prioritize rapid streaming performance and clean content delivery layout systems.

Secondary Segment / Brands and Partners

Corporate marketing managers, public relations executives, and advertising agency talent directors. They require quick access to commercial portfolios, host statistics, past activation highlights, and a friction-free booking funnel.

3. Styling

Design Tokens and Typography

The branding strongly relies on typographic scale rules and high visual graphics, intentionally omitting box structures or artificial background patterns
